English Coach · panel

Every state, light and dark, over a busy wallpaper and a code editor

Each panel is the delivered HTML file running in a frame at 100% scale, anchored bottom-right as it opens on screen. The blur behind it stands in for Windows 11 Acrylic, which the OS draws in the app. Open the interactive demo to switch states, themes and backdrops, and to resize.

Frosted glass
One tint over the whole panel: light #F3F3F3 at 82%, dark #202020 at 84%.
Regions
The result area and action row sit on the same glass, marked off by hairlines. Only the Natural version card adds a light veil (white at 45% in light, 5% in dark).
Worst-case contrast
Pure black behind light glass, pure white behind dark. Body text 10.3:1 and 8.8:1, secondary text 4.8:1 and 4.7:1, red, green and amber 4.7:1 or better.
Solid fallback
The same colours, opaque, when Windows transparency effects are off. Red is used only for struck-through original words.
